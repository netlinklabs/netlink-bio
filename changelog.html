Changelog

What's new in Netlink: updates, improvements, and fixes.

October 2026

New

41 templates for your Link in Bio

The template gallery now has 11 Silver templates and 30 Gold templates, and each one comes in a Light and a Dark look. Basic members use the Standard look (Light or Dark). Silver members can apply the 11 Silver templates. Gold members can apply all 41. Silver templates are clean and still. Gold templates add motion, glass, and glow.

New

Preview a template before you apply it

Tap any template to see your real profile with it, with a loading bar and a Light and Dark switch. Nothing changes until you tap Apply. Silver members can also preview every Gold template. Apply stays locked until you upgrade to Gold.

Improved

Light and Dark is now a simple switch

Choosing Light or Dark no longer removes your template. The Appearance switch only changes the look, and your template stays. Locked templates now show a clearer padlock, and animations turn off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

New

Certificate files and project links on your CV

Add each certification one by one and attach a PDF (max 2 MB) for Silver and Gold members. Visitors tap View Certificate to open it in a popup on your CV. Featured Projects now accept a link that opens in a new tab. Printed CVs also get cleaner margins on the second page.

New

Analytics for Silver and Gold members

See how many people visit your profile. Analytics shows the visits to your Link in Bio, CV, and Landing Page, so you know which one gets the most attention.

New

Verify your profile with Profile KYC

Profile KYC is now open to all members. Verify your profile to help visitors trust who is behind it.

New

Template gallery for Silver and Gold members

Choose from a wider range of templates for your Link in Bio page, so your profile can look the way you want.

New

Total Balance and hidden balances in Wallet

The main card now shows your Total Balance, which adds up your USDC and POL. NET is not included. You can also tap the eye icon to hide all your assets on screen for more privacy.

September 2026

New

AI Score for your profile and CV

See at a glance how complete your Link in Bio and CV are, and how easy they are for AI to read and recommend. Each gets its own 0-100 score with a quick breakdown of what's missing.

New

Reorder your CV sections

Work Experience, Education, Featured Projects, and Languages can now be reordered with a tap. Move any entry up or down to put your most important ones first.

Improved

Share your CV in one tap

Your CV now has its own Share button, right next to Copy and Preview near the top of the card, so there is no more digging around to send it to someone.

Improved

Clearer swap details before you confirm

The swap confirmation screen now shows the estimated network fee, where the fee is paid from, and exactly how much you'll receive. Details update live as the price refreshes, without the screen jumping around.

New

Swap with no POL in your wallet

Holding only USDC? You can now swap it to POL to get started. If your wallet has no free gas, the network fee is taken automatically from the token you're swapping, and the amount is adjusted so you're never left short.

New

Free gas on swaps for eligible members

Eligible members now swap between POL and USDC with the network fee covered by Netlink, just like sending.

Fixed

Swap success message showing when nothing was sent

Fixed an issue where "Swapped" could appear even though the transaction hadn't gone through. You'll now only see it after your swap is actually sent and recorded.

New

Local currency shown next to your balance

Your USDC and POL balances in Wallet now show an estimated value in your local currency right underneath, so you don't have to do the math yourself. Set your preferred currency from your profile settings.

New

Choose a Light or Dark look for your bio page

Basic tier members can now pick a Light or Dark template for their public bio page from the Dashboard's "Choose Template" card.

New

Welcome reward for completing your profile

Finish setting up your profile and claim your welcome NET reward from the new Rewards page. Track your progress and claim status right from there.

New

Free gas on Polygon for eligible members

Members on select tiers can now send NET, USDC, and POL on Polygon without needing any POL in their wallet. Netlink covers the network gas fee for you.

Improved

App Lock now protects your Wallet

The PIN/fingerprint lock has moved from the Dashboard to where it matters most: your Wallet and Account Recovery pages. It also checks in faster now: step away for more than 90 seconds and you'll be asked to unlock again.

Fixed

Several display issues on the Privacy & Data page

Fixed an issue where the PIN/fingerprint check could fail to appear when confirming account deletion, and fixed your "Verified Profile (KYC)" badge not showing in the page header.

Fixed

Wallet balance not loading

Fixed an issue where your wallet balance would sometimes fail to load, showing an error message instead of your balance. Balances now load faster and more reliably.

August 2026

Fixed

Stuck on the PIN screen

You can now cancel out of the PIN/fingerprint check (like when confirming account deletion) without being forced to log out. Tapping Cancel takes you right back to where you were.

Security

Safer wallet sessions

Added extra checks to make sure every send, backup, and export always uses the correct wallet session. This protects you if you're signed into more than one account on the same device.

Fixed

Send confirmation button

Fixed an issue where tapping "Confirm Send" quickly more than once could trigger the transaction twice.

Improved

Sign-in recovery flow

If your session ever needs to be refreshed, you can now sign in right where you are. No more being sent to another page and losing your place.

New

Emergency Export shows in your history

Assets moved through Emergency Export now appear in your transaction history, just like sends, receives, and swaps.

New

NET ID

Every Netlink account now has a permanent NET ID, a simple identifier you can share instead of your full wallet address.

Improved

Country field on your profile

Added a Country field to your profile, shown on your public bio and CV pages.

Earlier

New

Netlink Pay

Launched the built-in wallet. Hold USDC, NET, and POL, send and receive with anyone, and swap tokens directly in the app.

New

Verification badges

Introduced Green, Silver, Gold, and Black verification badges shown on your public profile.

New

Business landing pages

Added a landing page builder for business profiles with contact channels, gallery, and offerings, all editable from your dashboard.

Improved

Faster wallet recovery

Added backup sign-in options for your wallet, so losing access to one sign-in method doesn't mean losing access to your funds.
