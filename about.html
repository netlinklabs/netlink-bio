About Netlink

One trusted identity for everything — built for a world where people and AI both need to find, understand, and trust you online.

Our Mission

Netlink exists to give individuals and businesses one trusted digital identity — a profile that's easy for people to browse and accurate for AI to read.

Why Netlink Exists

Search is changing. AI assistants increasingly answer questions directly, without sending a click to a website — and AI agents are starting to compare people, services, and businesses on their own, reading structured data instead of browsing pages one by one. A profile built only for human eyes risks becoming invisible to this shift. Netlink builds every profile — bio, CV, and business page — with structured data from day one, so you're findable and accurately represented whether the reader is a person or an AI agent.
