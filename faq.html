Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about Netlink, NET Token, Netlink Pay, and crypto basics.

Netlink

What is Netlink?
Netlink is a digital identity platform. It combines a link-in-bio page, a professional digital CV, a business landing page, and a built-in crypto wallet — all designed with structured data so both people and AI can read your profile accurately.
How do I make my profile discoverable by AI?
Fill out your Netlink profile completely — bio, skills, experience, and portfolio. Netlink automatically adds structured data (JSON-LD) behind the scenes, so AI assistants can read and describe your profile accurately, not just display it visually.
How can I make my business discoverable by AI?
Create a Netlink Business Landing Page and select your business type. Each business type is automatically tagged with the correct schema (Store, Hotel, Travel Agency, and more), so AI assistants can accurately understand and recommend your business.
What are the benefits of being found by AI assistants?
AI assistants increasingly answer questions directly instead of showing a list of links. A profile with accurate structured data can be surfaced and recommended by AI when someone asks about your skills, services, or business — even without a traditional search click.
Why does AI matter for business and career today?
More people now ask AI assistants for recommendations instead of searching manually. If your profile or business isn't structured in a way AI can read, you become invisible in that growing channel — regardless of how good your work actually is.
What's the difference between Netlink and Linktree or other link-in-bio tools?
Most link-in-bio tools are just a list of links. Netlink adds a full digital CV, a business landing page, a crypto wallet, and AI-readable structured data on every page — built for discovery by both people and AI, not just clicks.
Is Netlink free to use?
Link in Bio, Digital CV, and Netlink Pay are free forever. Business Landing Page requires the Gold plan. Verification (Identity or Business) requires logging in and is not free.
What can I build with Netlink?
A Link in Bio page, a Digital CV, a Business Landing Page, a built-in crypto wallet (Netlink Pay), and verified trust badges through Identity or Business Verification.
Who operates Netlink?
Netlink is operated by PT Netlink Labs Global, a legally registered company in Indonesia.

NET Token

What is NET Token?
NET is a community utility token built on the Polygon blockchain, separate from the Netlink platform itself.
What's the difference between Netlink and NET Token?
Netlink is the platform — your profile, CV, business page, and wallet, operated by PT Netlink Labs Global. NET Token is a separate community token on Polygon. You don't need NET Token to use Netlink.
What blockchain is NET Token built on?
Polygon.
Do I need NET Token to use Netlink?
No. Netlink's core features — Link in Bio, Digital CV, and Netlink Pay — work without holding any NET Token.
Where can I learn more about NET Token?
Visit netlinktoken.com for details on NET Token.

Wallet

What is Netlink Pay?
Netlink Pay is a smart wallet built into every Netlink profile. Sign in with Google or email and your wallet is ready — no seed phrase required.
What is a NET ID?
A NET ID is a unique identifier (format NET-00000-00000) inside Netlink Pay. If you make it discoverable, others can send you payments using just your NET ID, without needing your full wallet address. A NET ID only works within Netlink Pay — it can't be used with other wallets.
Is Netlink Pay free?
Yes, Netlink Pay is free forever.
Do I need a seed phrase to use Netlink Pay?
No. Netlink Pay is a smart contract wallet with no seed phrase to write down or lose.
What happens if I lose access to my device — can I recover my wallet?
Yes, through Netlink's Trust Recovery process using your recovery email — not a seed phrase.
What currency does Netlink Pay support?
USDC on the Polygon network, along with POL and NET balances.
Does Netlink Pay cover my gas fees?
Netlink plans to sponsor gas fees for users on the Gold and Platinum plans, so sending funds won't require holding POL for gas. This feature is planned but not yet active — for now, all users pay standard Polygon network gas fees.

Crypto

What is a smart contract wallet?
A wallet secured by smart contract logic instead of a single private key you must store yourself — making recovery possible without a seed phrase.
What is Polygon?
Polygon is a fast, low-cost blockchain network. Netlink Pay and NET Token both run on Polygon.
What is USDC?
USDC is a stablecoin — a digital currency pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar.
Do I need to already understand crypto to use Netlink?
No. Netlink Pay is designed so you can sign in with Google or email and start using it immediately, without needing prior crypto knowledge.
Is Netlink Pay safe to use?
Yes. It's a smart contract wallet with no exportable private key, and account recovery goes through a verified Trust Recovery process instead of relying on a seed phrase.
