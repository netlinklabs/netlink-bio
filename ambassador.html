Ambassador Program

Pilot Season 1

Represent Netlink in your country and help build the community around it.

What ambassadors do

Ambassadors are the official face of Netlink in their country. Pick the track that fits you best. You do not need to recruit people to qualify.

  • Growth: bring new people to Netlink.
  • Content: create tutorials, videos and reviews.
  • Community: run and moderate a local group.
  • Influence: share Netlink with a wide audience.

How it works

  • Any Netlink member can apply. Identity verification is not required.
  • The Netlink team reviews every application manually, based on your Netlink bio page, your CV and what you share below. Complete both before you apply.
  • There is no fixed number of ambassadors per country.
  • Pilot Season 1 benefits are shared directly with approved ambassadors and may change.
  • Ambassador status is not employment and is not an investment. It can be ended by Netlink at any time.
