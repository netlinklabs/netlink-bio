This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Netlink.bio ("Netlink", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Netlink, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

Netlink.bio is operated by PT Netlink Labs Global, registered under NIB 0308260078406. Registered address: Jl. Karna Sosial No.128, Kelurahan Akcaya, Pontianak Selatan 78121, Indonesia. For privacy-related requests, contact contact@netlink.bio.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, username, avatar, password (via Supabase Auth)
  • Profile content: bio, CV, business page, links you add
  • Wallet data: your wallet address, and your wallet's linked Google email (if you create a smart wallet)
  • Verification data: identity/business verification status and documents, processed via our KYC/KYB partner
  • Contacts: contacts you save for payments
  • Usage data: local currency preference, theme preference, consent records
  • Technical data: IP address (captured when you accept a policy), device/browser information

We do not collect your wallet's private keys or seed phrase. Wallet creation and signing are handled by our wallet infrastructure partner (Sequence WaaS); we never have access to your keys.

3. How We Use Your Data, and Our Legal Basis

  • To provide and operate your Netlink profile, CV, business page, and wallet features — necessary to perform our contract with you
  • To verify your identity or business where you request verification — contract performance / legal obligation
  • To process token transfers and display transaction history — contract performance
  • To communicate service updates, rewards, and account notifications — legitimate interest
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations (e.g. fraud prevention, tax/audit requirements) — legal obligation

Where we rely on your consent for a specific purpose (such as accepting this policy), you may withdraw that consent at any time from your Privacy & Data dashboard — see Section 7.

4. Third Parties We Work With

We share limited data with service providers who help us operate Netlink:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Sequence WaaS — smart wallet infrastructure
  • LI.FI — token swap routing
  • Our KYC/KYB partner — identity and business verification
  • Alchemy Pay — fiat on/off-ramp (where used)

These providers only receive the data needed to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing purposes.

5. Blockchain Data

Netlink Pay transactions occur on the Polygon blockchain, which is public and immutable by design. Wallet addresses and transaction history recorded on-chain cannot be edited or deleted by us or by you — this is separate from the personal data stored in our own database.

6. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (including Supabase and Vercel) store or process data in the following regions: application hosting primarily in Singapore (Vercel), and database hosting in Sydney, Australia (Supabase). Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on our providers' standard contractual safeguards. If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, you can request more detail on these safeguards by contacting us using the details in Section 1.

7. Your Rights

From your Privacy & Data dashboard, you can:

  • Access / View a summary of the data categories we store about you
  • Export (data portability) your personal data as a downloadable file, in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Correct inaccurate profile data directly from your dashboard at any time
  • Manage consent and view your consent history, including withdrawing consent
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest (e.g. service communications) by contacting us
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances by contacting us
  • Request account deletion (erasure) — processed after a 45-day grace period, during which you may cancel the request

Some data (such as blockchain transaction records, or data we are legally required to retain) cannot be deleted even after account closure.

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe we have not handled your data lawfully.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

9. Security

We apply access controls (including database-level row security) so that your personal data is only accessible to you and to systems that need it to operate the service.

10. Changes to This Policy

When we make material changes to this policy, we will ask you to review and re-confirm your consent the next time you log in.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to contact@netlink.bio or via our support channel from within the app.
